# 📜 Elemental (Official Rulebook)

**A fast-paced, 5-minute set collection game for 2 to 4 players.**

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Game

Be the first player to collect **7 matching cards of any single element** in your face-up hand!

---

## 🃏 Game Setup

1. **The 38-Card Deck contains:**
* 🔥 **Fire:** 10 Cards
* 🌿 **Earth:** 9 Cards
* ⚡ **Air:** 9 Cards
* 💧 **Water:** 10 Cards


2. **Deal Starting Hands:** Shuffle the deck and deal **3 face-up cards** to each player.
3. **Set Up the Table:**
* All players keep their cards **face-up** in front of them so everyone can see each other's sets.
* Place the remaining deck face-down in the center.
* Leave room next to the deck for a single **face-up discard pile**.



---

## 🔄 Turn Structure

On your turn, perform these steps in order:

### 1. Mandatory Draw

Draw **1 card** from the deck and add it to your face-up hand.

> *If drawing this card gives you **7 matching elements**, you immediately win the game!*

### 2. Cast ONE Spell

Play 1 card from your hand face-up onto the discard pile to activate its elemental power (see Spells below). The discard-and-draw action is disabled in this version.

---

## ✨ The 4 Elemental Spells

When you play a spell card from your hand to the discard pile, trigger its effect immediately. After resolving the spell, draw 1 replacement card:

| Element | Spell Power |
| --- | --- |
| **🌿 Earth** | **Draw 2 Cards:** Draw 2 extra cards from the deck into your hand, then draw the 1 replacement card. |
| **⚡ Air** | **Steal 1 Card:** Choose 1 opponent and take 1 card from their hand, then draw the 1 replacement card. |
| **💧 Water** | **Retrieve 2 Cards:** Take up to 2 cards from the top of the discard pile into your hand. The Water spell you played stays on top. Then draw the 1 replacement card. |
| **🔥 Fire** | **Burn All Opponents:** Each opponent discards 2 cards of their choice, starting with the next player and proceeding in turn order. Then draw the 1 replacement card. |

---

## 🏆 Winning the Game

The game ends **instantly** the moment a player holds **7 cards of the exact same element** (e.g., 7 Fire, 7 Earth, 7 Water, or 7 Air). That player shouts *"Elemental!"* and wins!

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for Players

* **Keep an eye on Water:** The top card of the discard pile is always visible. If a card you need lands on top, cast a **Water** spell on your turn to scoop it up!
* **Protect your set from Fire:** When someone plays **Fire**, you get to pick which card you throw away—keep your matching set safe by discarding a card you don't need!
* **Watch opponent sets:** If an opponent is getting close to 7 cards, use **Fire** or **Air** to disrupt them before their next turn!
