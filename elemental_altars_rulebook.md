# 📜 Elemental Altars — Official Rulebook

> **Game Overview:** *Elemental Altars* is a fast-paced, highly interactive card game for 2–8 players (~10 turns per match). Players race to claim Mana on their Altars to fill their Vaults, sacrificing Mana fuel to cast game-warping elemental Spells.

---

## 🏆 Win Conditions

The game ends **immediately** at the end of any player's turn if their Vault reaches **either** threshold:

| Victory Path | Threshold Requirement | Strategic Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hoarder** | **46+ Points** total in Vault | Prioritizes staging high-value Mana cards (5, 6, 7). |
| **Collector** | **10+ Cards** total in Vault | Prioritizes fast volume, staging low Mana quickly to reach count parity. |

---

## 🔄 Turn Structure

On your turn, execute these **4 steps in order**:

1. **Claim Altar**
   * Move **all cards** currently on your Altar into your Vault. *(Vaulted cards are permanently secure and count toward victory.)*
2. **Stage Mana**
   * Select **1 Mana card** from your hand and place it face-up on your Altar.
   * *If you hold no Mana in hand:* Discard your entire hand to the Discard Pile, draw 5 new cards, and stage 1 Mana card if drawn.
3. **Cast Spell** *(Optional — Maximum 1 per turn)*
   * **Basic Spell:** Discard **1 Spell card** + **1 Mana card** (any value as fuel) to activate the **Basic Effect**.
   * **Enhanced Spell:** Discard **1 Spell card** + **2 Mana cards** (any value as fuel) to activate the **Enhanced Effect**.
   * Resolve the spell effect immediately.
4. **Draw to 5**
   * Refill your hand from the main deck until you hold exactly **5 cards**. *(If the deck is empty, shuffle the Discard Pile to form a new main deck.)*

---

## 🔥 Elemental Spells Reference

| Element | Basic Effect *(1 Spell + 1 Mana Fuel)* | Enhanced Effect *(1 Spell + 2 Mana Fuel)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🔥 **Fire** | **Target 1 Opponent:** Send 1 card from their Altar to the Discard Pile. | **Target ALL Opponents:** Send 1 card from each of their Altars to the Discard Pile. |
| 🌿 **Earth** | Place 1 Shield on your Altar. Blocks the **next incoming spell**. The shield expires at the start of your next turn. | Place 1 Shield on your Altar. Blocks **all incoming spells** until the start of your next turn. |
| 💧 **Water** | Choose **1 Mana card** from the Discard Pile and place it on your Altar. | Choose up to **2 Mana cards** from the Discard Pile and place them on your Altar. |
| ⚡ **Air** | **Choose 1 Opponent:** Steal their highest-value Mana card to your **Altar**. | **Choose 1 Opponent:** Steal up to their **2 highest-value Mana cards** to your **Altar**. |

## ⚙️ Setup & Deck Composition

* **Deck Composition (100 Cards Total):**
  * **60 Mana Cards:** Values 1–7 (10x1s, 10x2s, 10x3s, 10x4s, 8x5s, 6x6s, 6x7s).
  * **40 Spell Cards:** 10 Fire, 10 Earth, 10 Water, 10 Air.
* **Game Setup:**
  * Shuffle the 100-card deck.
  * Deal **5 cards** to each player.
  * The Discard Pile starts completely **empty (0 cards)**.
