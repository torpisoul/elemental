    How to play

    1. Everyone keeps their hand face-up. Deal 3 cards to each player.
    2. On your turn, draw 1 card. If you now have 7 of one element, you win immediately.
    3. Otherwise, take exactly one action: cast one card as its spell, or discard one card and draw a replacement.
    4. Your turn ends after that action. Shuffle the discard pile if the draw pile runs out.
