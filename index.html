      QUICK REFERENCE

      How to play

      1. Claim Altar. Move all Mana on your Altar into your Vault.
      2. Stage Mana. Place 1 Mana from your hand on your Altar. In this playtest, if you start your turn with fewer than 2 Mana, discard your hand and draw 5 once; keep the replacement even if it is still low, then stage 1 Mana if available.
      3. Cast a Spell (optional). Spend 1 Spell and either 1 Mana for its basic effect or 2 Mana for its enhanced effect. Maximum 1 Spell per turn.
      4. Draw to 5. Refill your hand. When the deck empties, shuffle the discard pile to make a new deck.

      🔥 FireDiscard 1 Mana from an opponent's Altar; enhanced hits all opponents.

      🌿 EarthBasic blocks the next spell targeting your Altar. Enhanced blocks every spell targeting your Altar until your next turn.

      💧 WaterChoose 1 or up to 2 Mana from the discard pile and add them to your Altar.

      ⚡ AirChoose an opponent and steal their highest Mana to your Altar; enhanced steals up to 2 highest Mana.

      First to 46+ Vault points or 10+ Vault cards wins at the end of their turn. Basic shields block the next spell targeting your Altar; enhanced shields block all such spells until your next turn.
